Test report

NodeWave runs on very different phones and every one has its own Bluetooth and Wi-Fi quirks. Work through the tests you can (nobody does them all), mark what happened, add the numbers asked for, and send the report. Most tests need two phones; a few need three. Your answers are saved in this browser as you go.
