Get involved

Help NodeWave grow

NodeWave is free software built by a very small team. It only becomes a network people can rely on when it has been tried on many different phones and when people know it exists. Here is how you can help, from a few minutes to a few euro.

Lend a device

The more models we can hold in our hands, the fewer surprises at release time. If you have an Android phone you no longer use (any brand, Android 10 or newer, Bluetooth LE) or a LoRa board, you can make it available for testing. Open an issue to get in touch and tell us the model. See which phones are already covered.

Test on your own phone

Install NodeWave on two phones, go through the test list and fill in the report form. Bluetooth behaves differently on every model, so a single report from a phone we do not have is valuable.

Report a problem

Something broke or felt wrong? Open an issue saying what you did, what you expected and which phone and Android version you use.

Tell your friends

A mesh needs people nearby. Talk about NodeWave with friends, family, hiking and sailing groups, volunteers, festival crews: anyone who goes where there is no signal. Point them to nodewave.org.

Share it online

Post about it on your social networks and mention nodewave.org as the home of the project, so people land on the real download and documentation.

Join the communities

Take part in forums, groups and discussions about off-grid communication, mesh networking, LoRa, privacy, hiking and emergency preparedness. Share your experience, answer questions, and when it fits, introduce NodeWave with a link to nodewave.org. Please be honest and not pushy: NodeWave is young and has known limits.

Contribute code or translations

The source is on GitHub under the AGPL-3.0-or-later licence. Star the repository, read the code, and send a pull request.

Make a donation

Money helps cover development time, test phones, radio boards and hosting. Nothing is locked behind it. Donate with PayPal, any amount is welcome. Every contribution and every expense is listed on the transparency page.

Ready to start?

The quickest way to help today takes ten minutes and two phones.
