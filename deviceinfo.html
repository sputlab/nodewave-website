Device report

Bluetooth behaves differently on every phone. The device report tells us which phone NodeWave runs on and what its radios can do, so problems can be matched to hardware and fixed. Paste it below and send it: no email address of yours is needed.

Nothing personal

The report describes the phone, never you.

It holds no personal data, no identifiers and no codes of any kind. You can read every word of it before sending, here and in the app.

What it contains

  • Brand, model, chipset, memory and screen of the phone
  • Android version, API level and security patch date
  • What Bluetooth and Wi-Fi can do (PHYs, advertising, Wi-Fi Direct)
  • NodeWave version, radio state, how many nodes are in reach
  • Which permissions NodeWave holds, battery optimisation on or off

What it never contains

  • Your name, the node name, the node ID or any key
  • Messages, contacts, groups, positions or the map
  • IMEI, serial numbers, Android ID or advertising ID
  • MAC or IP addresses, phone number, accounts
  • Wi-Fi network names, installed apps, language or time zone

Your report

NodeWave copied it when you tapped Copy and open the web form. Paste it here: long-press the box and choose Paste, or use the button.

Nothing is sent until you press Send report.

Thank you

The report is on its way. It helps NodeWave work on one more kind of phone.

Back to nodewave.org

Don't have the report yet?

  1. Open NodeWave (version 0.15 or later) on the phone.
  2. Go to Settings → Device report and read what it contains.
  3. Tap Copy and open the web form: this page opens and the report is ready to paste.

You can also choose Send by email in the app, which sends the same text from your own email app to nodewave_device_info@sputlab.com. This page goes to the same address without involving your email account: the text passes through FormSubmit, a form-to-email service, which forwards it to us. Like any website, the service sees the connection it comes from; the report itself carries nothing personal. Reports are read only to improve NodeWave and are never published with anything that could identify the sender.
